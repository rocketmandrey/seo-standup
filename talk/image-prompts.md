# Промпты для трёх иллюстраций

Иллюстрации без текста внутри (максимум 2–3 короткие подписи):
метафору рассказывает спикер, картинка не превращается в слайд с
инфографикой. Если нужно ужаться до двух — оставляй рынок + сеть, а
цикл рисуй на последнем слайде четырьмя словами:
**Спрос → Страница → Данные → Улучшение → ↻**

## 1. Рынок / поисковик

> Minimal editorial schematic illustration, horizontal 16:9.
> A large friendly market stall seen from the front.
> Behind the counter is one shopkeeper representing a search engine.
> In front of the stall, several customers are asking for different
> kinds of apples: red apples, green apples, small apples, large apples.
> Behind the shopkeeper are neatly organized trays of different fruits.
> One farmer approaches from the side carrying crates of apples to
> supply the shop.
> Important visual metaphor: customers = search demand, shopkeeper =
> search engine, farmer = website owner, fruit = content/products.
> Show that the shopkeeper connects customer demand with the most
> appropriate product.
> Simple geometric shapes, clean editorial vector illustration,
> white/light background, limited visual detail, no logos, no text,
> no UI elements, easy to understand from a presentation screen.

## 2. Сайт как сеть — главный визуал выступления

> Conceptual editorial illustration, horizontal 16:9.
> An ocean full of many fish of different sizes.
> Large fish represent high-volume search queries, medium and tiny fish
> represent long-tail search queries.
> A carefully designed fishing net is spread through the water.
> The net has different sections and mesh sizes, strategically
> positioned to catch different groups of fish.
> Some fish are large, some small, some swim in clusters.
> The key metaphor must be immediately clear: the ocean = total search
> demand, fish = search queries, the architecture of the fishing net =
> website structure.
> The net should look intentionally engineered rather than randomly
> thrown into the ocean.
> A few groups of fish should pass through obvious gaps in poorly
> covered areas, showing missed demand.
> Clean schematic editorial illustration, simple geometry, light
> background, no text, no labels, no logos, presentation quality.

## 3. SEO как feedback loop

> Minimal schematic editorial illustration, horizontal 16:9.
> Show a circular continuous system with four visual stages connected
> by arrows.
> Stage 1: many people expressing different needs/questions.
> Stage 2: a website/page being created to answer those needs.
> Stage 3: people arriving at the page from a search engine.
> Stage 4: data/signals flowing back and the website being improved.
> Then the loop returns to the users/search demand.
> The website in the second pass should visibly become slightly more
> complete and better structured, emphasizing continuous improvement.
> Core metaphor: SEO is not publishing once, but a feedback loop
> between real search demand, content, user behavior, data, and
> improvement.
> Very clean editorial vector diagram, simple geometric objects, lots
> of whitespace, no text, no numbers, no logos, no realistic UI
> screenshots.
